PRIVACY
POLICY.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Veyra may collect, why it is used, and how it is handled across the Veyra website, launcher, client, and related services.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Depending on the feature you use, Veyra may process information such as Minecraft or Discord identifiers, launcher/client diagnostics, crash information, configuration data, download/update information, and information you voluntarily provide for support or community features.

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

Information may be used to operate Veyra, deliver downloads and updates, provide support, prevent abuse, maintain security, diagnose crashes and technical issues, and improve the software.

3. LAUNCHER AND CLIENT DATA

The Veyra launcher may process local installation, profile, version, mod, resource-pack, shader, and configuration information required to manage your Veyra installation. Local Minecraft data remains on your device unless a feature explicitly transmits information to a service.

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Veyra may use third-party services for downloads, distribution, authentication, hosting, analytics, or community functionality. Examples may include Modrinth, CurseForge, GitHub, Discord, and infrastructure providers. Their respective privacy policies apply to information they process.

5. DATA RETENTION

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, security, legal obligations, or legitimate operational requirements.

6. SECURITY

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

Where applicable, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with Veyra services. Requests may be subject to verification and applicable legal requirements.

8. CHILDREN

Veyra is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. CHANGES

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Veyra's services or legal requirements change. The current version published at this URL applies to continued use of the services.

10. CONTACT

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Veyra team through the official Veyra community/support channels.
